Privacy notice

Last updated 22 August 2026

The short version. If you send us an enquiry we keep your name, email, organisation and message so we can reply. We count page visits without cookies. We do not sell anything to anyone, and we do not run advertising. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

For the information described on this page we are the data controller. Where a customer uses our payroll software to process their own employees' data, that customer is the controller and we act as their processor — see the data processing agreement.

Data protection enquiries: use the form on our contact section and mark your message "data protection".

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Our analytics provider measures visits without cookies and without building a profile of you across sites, which is why you are not being asked to accept anything.

Data leaving the UK

Enquiry form submissions are processed in the United States by Formspree, and reach us by email through Google. Both providers rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

This applies only to enquiries sent through this website. It does not apply to payroll data belonging to customers of our software, which is held in the United Kingdom — see security.

How long we keep things

Enquiries are deleted 24 months after we last heard from you, unless you become a customer, in which case the customer contract governs retention. Server logs are cleared within 30 days. Analytics are aggregated and hold nothing that identifies you.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of what we hold about you
  • correct anything inaccurate
  • delete it
  • restrict what we do with it, or object to us using it
  • provide it in a portable format

We answer within one month. There is no charge unless a request is clearly excessive. Because we rely on legitimate interests for enquiry data, you can object at any time and we will stop unless we have a compelling reason not to, which for a sales enquiry we will not.

Complaints

Please raise it with us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions about you by automated means on this website. Within our payroll software, no individual is scored, ranked or profiled, and no automated process determines anyone's pay — a named person approves every payroll run before payment is made.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant and we hold your contact details, we will tell you directly.
